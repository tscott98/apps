# Cambrian

A screensaver of a Cambrian sea, 508 million years ago.

Every critter is drawn from code on every frame, not played back from a recording:

- **Anomalocaris** ripples its swimming flaps and curls its grasping claws.
- **Opabinia** sweeps its long proboscis and snaps the claw at its tip.
- **Hallucigenia** walks on seven pairs of tentacle legs.
- **Olenoides** (a trilobite) patters along the seafloor, feeling ahead with its antennae.
- **Marrella** flashes iridescent head spines.
- **Wiwaxia** glints with scales and sways its spines.
- **Pikaia** undulates through the water.
- **Comb jellies** ripple rainbow light down their comb rows.

Each run picks a fresh seafloor of Vauxia sponges, Choia and algae, under shafts of light and drifting marine snow.

## Settings

- **Featured critter:** the critter that swims across the scene, or Random.
- **Other critters:** how many other critters share the scene (None, Some or Lots).
- **Show name:** show the featured critter's name at the start.
